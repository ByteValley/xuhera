隐私政策

序和（XUHERA）· 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日（更新）

首页English

本政策说明“序和”iPhone 应用（以下称“本应用”）如何处理你的数据。简要来说：本应用没有自己的服务器，不收集、不出售你的数据，也不含广告或数据分析工具。数据保存在你的设备、Apple“健康”和你自己选择的云服务中。

1. 本应用处理哪些数据

Apple“健康”（HealthKit）

经你授权后，本应用会读取睡眠阶段、睡眠期间的心率、心率变异性、呼吸频率、血氧和手腕温度、体重、体脂率、去脂体重、BMI、身高、出生日期和性别，用于显示趋势和生成摘要；也会把体重、体脂率、去脂体重、BMI 以及你记录的饮食热量和营养素写入“健康”。“健康”数据不会用于广告，不会出售，也不会提供给第三方，除非是你在下文第 2 节中主动选择的服务。

你在应用内输入的内容

复诊记录（症状、医生方案、处方、备注）、睡眠与症状摘要、家庭成员资料（名字、身高、出生年份、性别）、身体测量记录、目标和饮食记录。这些内容保存在本机。

蓝牙体脂秤

本应用通过蓝牙读取体脂秤的体重和阻抗，并把身高、年龄和性别发送给秤，以便测量体脂。连接日志只保存在本机，你可以自行导出或清空。

相机

你选择拍照记录饮食时，照片用于识别食物和热量（见第 2 节 AI 分析）。

2. 数据会发送到哪里（均由你主动开启）

  • AI 分析：本应用优先使用设备上的本机模型，数据不离开设备。只有在你确认后，才会把当次分析所需的内容（例如摘要中的睡眠明细、症状、医生方案、处方和备注，身体数据，或饮食照片）发送给你选择的服务：OpenAI（通过你登录的 ChatGPT 账号计划）或你自己配置的备用 API。这些服务按其各自的隐私政策处理数据。
  • iCloud 同步：就诊记录、用药、睡眠日记、摘要、身体数据和应用设置在上传前先在本机端到端加密，再保存到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库。加密密钥自动保存在你的 iCloud 钥匙串中（同样是端到端加密），只有你登录同一 Apple ID 的设备能取得；iCloud 中只保存加密后的内容，Apple、本应用开发者和其他任何人都无法读取。
  • Google Drive 同步（中国大陆商店不提供）：数据先在本机端到端加密，再上传到你 Google Drive 的“应用专属隐藏空间”（appDataFolder）。本应用看不到你 Google Drive 中的其他文件。
  • PikPak WebDAV 同步（中国大陆商店不提供）：数据同样先在本机端到端加密，再上传到你填写的 WebDAV 地址。

所有同步方式使用同一把加密密钥：默认由本应用随机生成，只保存在你的 iCloud 钥匙串中（端到端加密），仅你登录同一 Apple ID 的设备可以取得；你也可以改为自己设置的同步密码，它只保存在各设备的钥匙串中。密钥和密码都不会上传到任何云盘。Google 和 PikPak 的登录凭据保存在本机钥匙串中，不会上传。

3. 数据存放地点

本应用开发者没有服务器，不保存你的任何数据。你的数据只存放在以下位置：

  • 你的设备：应用数据保存在 iPhone 上本应用的沙盒中，API 密钥和登录凭据保存在本机钥匙串中。
  • Apple“健康”：由 Apple 管理，保存在你的设备上；如你开启了“健康”的 iCloud 同步，也由 Apple 保存在 iCloud 中。
  • iCloud（如开启）：保存在你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库中，且为端到端加密。中国大陆 Apple 账户的 iCloud 服务由云上贵州运营，数据存放在中国境内；其他地区由 Apple 运营。
  • PikPak（如开启，中国大陆商店不提供）：加密后保存在你填写的 PikPak WebDAV 服务器上，存放地点由 PikPak 决定。
  • Google Drive（如开启，中国大陆商店不提供）：加密后保存在 Google 的服务器上，可能位于中国境外。
  • AI 分析：本机模型的分析不离开设备。自定义 API 会把你确认发送的内容传给你选择的服务商，存放地点由该服务商决定（国内服务商通常在中国境内）；ChatGPT / OpenAI（中国大陆商店不提供）位于中国境外。

4. Google 用户数据

你用 Google 账号登录时，本应用只申请以下权限：

  • openid 和 email：在设置中显示你登录的是哪个 Google 账号；
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata：在你 Google Drive 的应用专属隐藏空间中读写本应用的加密同步文件。

这些数据只用于为你提供同步功能。本应用不会把从 Google 获得的数据用于广告，不会出售，不会转交他人，也不会用于训练 AI 模型；除非你要求，人工不会查看这些数据。

本应用使用和向任何其他应用传输从 Google API 获得的信息时，将遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”（Limited Use）要求。

5. 保存、删除与撤销

  • 你可以在应用内删除复诊记录、摘要、身体记录和饮食记录；删除会在下次同步时传到你的其他设备。
  • 删除本应用会清除本机数据。“健康”中的数据可在“健康”App 中管理。
  • 云端副本可以在 iCloud、Google Drive（设置 → 管理应用）或 PikPak 中删除。
  • 你可以随时在应用中退出 Google 账号，或在 Google 账号权限页面撤销本应用的访问权限。
  • 首次使用时，你会单独同意本应用处理健康信息（敏感个人信息）。你可以随时在 iPhone 的“设置 → 健康 → 数据访问与设备”中关闭本应用的读取和写入权限，以撤回这项同意；撤回不影响此前基于同意已进行的处理。
  • 你依法享有查阅、复制、更正、删除你的个人信息及撤回同意等权利。由于数据都保存在你自己的设备和云服务中，你可以直接在应用、“健康”App 或相应云服务中行使；如需帮助，请通过下方邮箱联系。

6. 医疗声明

本应用的睡眠分期、身体成分和 AI 内容均为估算或参考，不构成诊断或治疗建议，请与医生核对。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集他们的个人信息。

8. 政策变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会更新本页面和上方的生效日期。如对本政策或你的个人信息有任何问题、意见或请求，请联系：bytevalley@outlook.com

Privacy Policy (English)

XUHERA · Effective November 23, 2026

XUHERA has no servers of its own. It does not collect or sell your data and contains no ads or analytics. Your data stays on your device, in Apple Health, and in cloud services you choose.

Data the app handles

  • Apple Health (HealthKit): with your permission, the app reads sleep stages, heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen and wrist temperature during sleep, weight, body fat, lean body mass, BMI, height, date of birth, and sex to show trends and create summaries, and writes weight, body composition, and logged nutrition to Health. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties except the services you choose below.
  • Content you enter: visit notes, summaries, family members' profiles, body measurements, goals, and meals, stored on your device.
  • Bluetooth scales: the app reads weight and impedance from your scale and sends it your height, age, and sex so it can measure body fat. The connection log stays on your device.
  • Camera: meal photos you take are used to recognize food and calories.

Where data goes (only when you turn it on)

  • AI analysis: on-device first. Only after you confirm does the app send the content needed for that analysis to the service you chose: OpenAI (through your ChatGPT plan) or a backup API you configured. Those services handle data under their own policies.
  • iCloud sync: visit notes, medications, sleep diary entries, summaries, body data, and app settings are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they are stored in your private iCloud database. The encryption key is kept automatically in your iCloud Keychain (itself end-to-end encrypted) and is available only to your devices signed in to the same Apple ID. iCloud holds only ciphertext that Apple, the developer, and anyone else cannot read.
  • Google Drive sync (not offered on the mainland China App Store): end-to-end encrypted on your device, then stored in the hidden app data folder of your Google Drive. The app cannot see your other Drive files.
  • PikPak WebDAV sync (not offered on the mainland China App Store): end-to-end encrypted on your device, then uploaded to the WebDAV address you enter.

All sync methods use one encryption key: by default generated randomly by the app and kept only in your iCloud Keychain (end-to-end encrypted), available to your devices signed in to the same Apple ID; you can instead set your own sync password, kept only in each device's Keychain. Neither is ever uploaded to any cloud drive. Google and PikPak credentials stay in this device's Keychain and are never uploaded.

Where data is stored

The developer has no servers and stores none of your data. It is kept only in these places:

  • Your device: app data in the app's sandbox on your iPhone; API keys and sign-in credentials in the device Keychain.
  • Apple Health: managed by Apple on your device, and in iCloud if you sync Health with iCloud.
  • iCloud (if on): your own private iCloud database, end-to-end encrypted. For Apple Accounts in mainland China, iCloud is operated by GCBD and data is stored in China; elsewhere it is operated by Apple.
  • PikPak (if on; not offered on the mainland China App Store): encrypted, on the PikPak WebDAV server you enter; PikPak decides where it is located.
  • Google Drive (if on; not offered on the mainland China App Store): encrypted, on Google's servers, which may be outside China.
  • AI analysis: the on-device model never leaves the device. A custom API receives what you confirm sending, stored wherever that provider keeps it. ChatGPT / OpenAI (not offered on the mainland China App Store) are outside China.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, the app requests only openid and email (to show which account is signed in) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (to store the app's encrypted sync file in your Drive's app data folder). This data is used only to provide sync. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to others, not used to train AI models, and not read by humans unless you ask.

XUHERA's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion, and revoking access

You can delete records in the app; deletions sync to your other devices. Deleting the app removes its local data; Health data is managed in the Health app; cloud copies can be deleted in iCloud, Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps), or PikPak. You can sign out of Google in the app or revoke access at your Google Account permissions.

On first use you give separate consent to processing your health information (sensitive personal information). You can withdraw it at any time by turning off the app's access in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices; this does not affect processing already done with your consent. You have the right to access, copy, correct and delete your personal information and to withdraw consent. As your data is kept on your own devices and cloud services, you can do this directly in the app, the Health app or the cloud service; for help, contact us at the address below.

Medical disclaimer, children, changes, contact

Sleep stages, body composition, and AI content are estimates for reference, not diagnosis or treatment advice. The app is not directed at children under 13.
