隐私政策

序和（XUHERA）· 生效日期：2026 年 11 月 28 日（更新）

首页English

本政策说明“序和”iPhone 应用（以下称“本应用”）如何处理你的数据。简要来说：本应用没有自己的服务器，不收集、不出售你的数据，也不含广告或数据分析工具。数据保存在你的设备、Apple“健康”和你自己选择的云服务中。

1. 本应用处理哪些数据

Apple“健康”（HealthKit）

经你授权后，本应用会读取睡眠阶段、睡眠期间的心率、心率变异性、呼吸频率、血氧和手腕温度、体重、体脂率、去脂体重、BMI、身高、出生日期和性别，用于显示趋势和生成摘要；还会读取每日的心率、静息心率、心率变异性、步行平均心率、最大摄氧量、血氧、呼吸频率、步数、步行距离、活动能量、锻炼和站立时间、耳机音量、睡眠呼吸紊乱、日光时长、正念时长、体能训练记录和心电图结果，以及你在“健康”中记录的心情、经期、咖啡因和饮酒（性行为记录仅在你于“私密健康”中开启后读取，见下文），用于在本机计算综合评分（睡眠、恢复、活动、听觉）和生理压力，并在“身体”页显示近 30 天趋势；经期仅用于在经期前后减弱心率对压力分的影响，心情仅用于和压力一起显示。评分只在本机计算和显示。这些每日数据默认不会写入摘要、不会同步，也不会发送给 AI 服务；只有当你在生成就诊摘要时主动开启“附上心率、活动与心电图”，所选周期的每周静息心率、心率变异性、步数、锻炼、睡眠呼吸紊乱、血氧和心电图结果才会写入该摘要，并随摘要按第 2 节同步，在你确认联网 AI 分析时一并发送；心电图只读取分类结果和平均心率，不读取波形；也会把体重、体脂率、去脂体重、BMI 以及你记录的饮食热量和营养素写入“健康”。“健康”数据不会用于广告，不会出售，也不会提供给第三方，除非是你在下文第 2 节中主动选择的服务。

你在应用内输入的内容

复诊记录（症状、医生方案、处方、备注、下次复诊日期和想问医生的问题）、用药与服药打卡、药物副作用记录、量表答案和分数（如 PHQ-9、GAD-7，以及 ISI、ESS、PSQI、PROMIS 睡眠量表、SCI 和 STOP-Bang 等睡眠量表；STOP-Bang 包含关于打鼾、高血压、BMI、年龄、颈围和性别的是/否回答）、睡眠日记、睡眠窗口计划、睡眠与症状摘要、家庭成员资料（名字、身高、出生年份、性别）、身体测量记录、目标和饮食记录。这些内容保存在本机；小组件和 Apple Watch 上显示的睡眠与用药信息也只保存在你自己的设备上。

蓝牙体脂秤

本应用通过蓝牙读取体脂秤的体重和阻抗，并把身高、年龄和性别发送给秤，以便测量体脂。连接日志只保存在本机，你可以自行导出或清空。

相机

你选择拍照记录饮食时，照片用于识别食物和热量（见第 2 节 AI 分析）。你拍摄化验单或处方时，文字识别在设备上完成，照片不会保存，也不会上传。

私密健康（性生活记录与男性功能自评）

“私密健康”页可手动记录性生活的时间、是否采取保护措施、满意度和备注，并可填写国际勃起功能指数简表（IIEF-5）和早泄诊断工具（PEDT）。经你在该页的隐私设置中单独授权后，本应用会读取 Apple“健康”中的性行为记录（含是否采取保护措施），并可按你的选择把手动记录的时间和保护措施写入“健康”（不写入备注和满意度）。这些属于敏感个人信息，处理方式如下：

  • 保存在本机并使用 iOS 完全文件保护；可选开启面容 ID、触控 ID 或设备密码锁，可选在“身体”页隐藏入口。
  • 只随第 2 节所述端到端加密的同步包同步到你自己的其他设备，不写入可读的 iCloud 快照，不会出现在小组件、Apple Watch、就诊摘要或 PDF 中。
  • 家人共享默认不包含；你开启后，只共享近 30 天的次数和最新自评的分数与分级，不共享具体记录或备注。
  • 默认不用于 AI 分析；你开启后，只发送统计结果（次数、保护比例、与睡眠/压力/运动的关联和自评分数，不含备注和具体日期），使用联网服务前每次确认。
  • 导出 CSV 时默认不包含，需每次单独勾选；自评提醒通知不显示具体主题。
  • 与睡眠、压力、运动的关联分析和自评分级只在本机计算，仅供参考，不是医疗诊断。

日历与相册

你选择“添加到日历”时，本应用只写入下次复诊的日程（含提醒和想问医生的问题），不读取你的日历。你保存分享图片时，本应用只向相册添加图片，不读取你的照片。

2. 数据会发送到哪里（均由你主动开启）

  • AI 分析：AI 分析默认“自动选择”方式：你已连接 ChatGPT 账号计划或配置了自定义 API 时优先使用它们，否则使用设备上的本机模型（数据不离开设备）；你也可以在设置中固定只用本机模型。使用联网方式时，只有在你确认后，才会把当次分析所需的内容（例如摘要中的睡眠明细、症状、医生方案、处方和备注、量表分数、服药依从性和副作用，身体数据，或饮食照片），发送前会在确认页列出发送给你选择的服务：OpenAI（通过你登录的 ChatGPT 账号计划）或你自己配置的备用 API。这些服务按其各自的隐私政策处理数据。
  • iCloud 同步：就诊记录、用药、睡眠日记、摘要、身体数据和应用设置在上传前先在本机端到端加密，再保存到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库。加密密钥自动保存在你的 iCloud 钥匙串中（同样是端到端加密），只有你登录同一 Apple ID 的设备能取得；iCloud 中只保存加密后的内容，Apple、本应用开发者和其他任何人都无法读取。
  • Google Drive 同步（中国大陆商店不提供）：数据先在本机端到端加密，再上传到你 Google Drive 的“应用专属隐藏空间”（appDataFolder）。本应用看不到你 Google Drive 中的其他文件。
  • PikPak WebDAV 同步（中国大陆商店不提供）：数据同样先在本机端到端加密，再上传到你填写的 WebDAV 地址。
  • 家人代看：你可以主动邀请家人查看一份只读的睡眠概况：最近 30 晚的睡眠时长与评分、正在使用的药物与服药打卡、下次复诊日期、量表分数，以及最近一次就诊的症状、医生方案和处方要点。共享通过 Apple 的 iCloud（CloudKit）共享完成，数据仍保存在你自己的 iCloud 中，内容字段加密保存，本应用开发者无法读取。家人只能查看、不能修改；你可以随时移除某位家人或停止共享，停止后家人设备上的副本会自动删除。
  • 分享与导出：分享图片、睡眠日记 PDF、就诊摘要和 CSV 数据导出只在你主动操作时生成，并通过系统分享面板发送到你选择的地方。

所有同步方式使用同一把加密密钥：默认由本应用随机生成，只保存在你的 iCloud 钥匙串中（端到端加密），仅你登录同一 Apple ID 的设备可以取得；你也可以改为自己设置的同步密码，它只保存在各设备的钥匙串中。密钥和密码都不会上传到任何云盘。Google 和 PikPak 的登录凭据保存在本机钥匙串中，不会上传。

3. 数据存放地点

本应用开发者没有服务器，不保存你的任何数据。你的数据只存放在以下位置：

  • 你的设备：应用数据保存在 iPhone 上本应用的沙盒中，API 密钥和登录凭据保存在本机钥匙串中。
  • Apple“健康”：由 Apple 管理，保存在你的设备上；如你开启了“健康”的 iCloud 同步，也由 Apple 保存在 iCloud 中。
  • iCloud（如开启）：保存在你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库中，且为端到端加密。中国大陆 Apple 账户的 iCloud 服务由云上贵州运营，数据存放在中国境内；其他地区由 Apple 运营。
  • PikPak（如开启，中国大陆商店不提供）：加密后保存在你填写的 PikPak WebDAV 服务器上，存放地点由 PikPak 决定。
  • Google Drive（如开启，中国大陆商店不提供）：加密后保存在 Google 的服务器上，可能位于中国境外。
  • AI 分析：本机模型的分析不离开设备。自定义 API 会把你确认发送的内容传给你选择的服务商，存放地点由该服务商决定（国内服务商通常在中国境内）；ChatGPT / OpenAI（中国大陆商店不提供）位于中国境外。

4. Google 用户数据

你用 Google 账号登录时，本应用只申请以下权限：

  • openid 和 email：在设置中显示你登录的是哪个 Google 账号；
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata：在你 Google Drive 的应用专属隐藏空间中读写本应用的加密同步文件。

这些数据只用于为你提供同步功能。本应用不会把从 Google 获得的数据用于广告，不会出售，不会转交他人，也不会用于训练 AI 模型；除非你要求，人工不会查看这些数据。

本应用使用和向任何其他应用传输从 Google API 获得的信息时，将遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的“有限使用”（Limited Use）要求。

5. 保存、删除与撤销

  • 你可以在应用内删除复诊记录、摘要、身体记录和饮食记录；删除会在下次同步时传到你的其他设备。
  • 删除本应用会清除本机数据。“健康”中的数据可在“健康”App 中管理。
  • 云端副本可以在 iCloud、Google Drive（设置 → 管理应用）或 PikPak 中删除。
  • 你可以随时在应用中退出 Google 账号，或在 Google 账号权限页面撤销本应用的访问权限。
  • 首次使用时，你会单独同意本应用处理健康信息（敏感个人信息）。你可以随时在 iPhone 的“设置 → 健康 → 数据访问与设备”中关闭本应用的读取和写入权限，以撤回这项同意；撤回不影响此前基于同意已进行的处理。
  • 你依法享有查阅、复制、更正、删除你的个人信息及撤回同意等权利。由于数据都保存在你自己的设备和云服务中，你可以直接在应用、“健康”App 或相应云服务中行使；如需帮助，请通过下方邮箱联系。

6. 医疗声明

本应用的睡眠分期、身体成分、睡眠量表（如 ISI、ESS、PSQI、STOP-Bang）的分级、私密健康的分析与 IIEF-5、PEDT 自评分级以及 AI 内容均为估算或参考，不构成诊断或治疗建议，请与医生核对；如有性功能方面的困扰，请咨询泌尿外科、男科或性医学门诊。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集他们的个人信息。

8. 政策变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会更新本页面和上方的生效日期。如对本政策或你的个人信息有任何问题、意见或请求，请联系：bytevalley@outlook.com

Privacy Policy (English)

XUHERA · Effective November 28, 2026

XUHERA has no servers of its own. It does not collect or sell your data and contains no ads or analytics. Your data stays on your device, in Apple Health, and in cloud services you choose.

Data the app handles

  • Apple Health (HealthKit): with your permission, the app reads sleep stages, heart rate, heart rate variability, respiratory rate, blood oxygen and wrist temperature during sleep, weight, body fat, lean body mass, BMI, height, date of birth, and sex to show trends and create summaries. It also reads daily heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, walking heart rate, VO₂ max, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, steps, walking distance, active energy, exercise and stand time, headphone audio levels, breathing disturbances, time in daylight, mindful minutes, workouts, and ECG results, plus the mood, periods, caffeine, and alcohol you log in Health, to work out overview scores (sleep, recovery, activity, hearing) and physiological stress on your device and show 30-day trends on the Body tab. Periods are used only to soften the heart-rate part of the stress score around a period, and mood is only shown next to stress. Scores are calculated and shown only on your device. By default these daily data are not added to summaries, not synced, and not sent to AI services. Only if you turn on "Attach heart, activity and ECG" when creating a visit summary are the period's weekly resting heart rate, heart rate variability, steps, exercise, breathing disturbances, blood oxygen and ECG results added to that summary; they then sync with it as described below and are sent with it when you confirm an online AI analysis. For ECGs, only the classification and average heart rate are read, not the waveform. The app writes weight, body composition, and logged nutrition to Health. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with third parties except the services you choose below.
  • Content you enter: visit notes (including the next visit date and questions for your doctor), medications and dose check-ins, side-effect logs, questionnaire answers and scores (such as PHQ-9 and GAD-7, and sleep scales including ISI, ESS, PSQI, PROMIS sleep, SCI and STOP-Bang; STOP-Bang includes yes/no answers about snoring, high blood pressure, BMI, age, neck size and sex), sleep diary entries, your sleep window plan, summaries, family members' profiles, body measurements, goals, and meals, stored on your device. Sleep and medication details shown in widgets and on Apple Watch also stay on your own devices.
  • Bluetooth scales: the app reads weight and impedance from your scale and sends it your height, age, and sex so it can measure body fat. The connection log stays on your device.
  • Camera: meal photos you take are used to recognize food and calories. Photos of lab reports or prescriptions are read on the device; the photo is not saved or uploaded.
  • Private Health (sexual activity and male sexual function): on the Private Health page you can log the time of sexual activity, whether protection was used, satisfaction and a note, and fill in the IIEF-5 and PEDT questionnaires. Only if you turn it on in that page's privacy settings does the app read sexual activity (including whether protection was used) from Apple Health, and, if you choose, save the time and protection of records you log to Health (never the note or satisfaction). This is sensitive personal information: it is stored on your device with complete file protection, can be locked with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode and hidden from the Body tab, and syncs only inside the end-to-end encrypted package to your own devices. It never appears in the readable iCloud snapshot, widgets, Apple Watch, visit summaries or PDFs. It is not shared with family unless you turn that on, and then only the count over 30 days and the latest questionnaire scores and grades. It is not used for AI analysis unless you turn that on, and then only statistics are sent (no notes or dates), with your confirmation each time for online services. CSV exports leave it out unless you tick it, and reminders never name the topic.
  • Calendar and Photos: "Add to Calendar" only writes your next visit (with reminders and questions for your doctor) and never reads your calendar. Saving a share image only adds it to your photo library and never reads your photos.

Where data goes (only when you turn it on)

  • AI analysis: by default the app chooses automatically: a ChatGPT plan you connected or a custom API you configured is used first, otherwise the on-device model (data stays on the device); you can also choose the on-device model only in Settings. With an online method, only after you confirm does the app send the content needed for that analysis (listed on the confirmation screen, such as sleep details, symptoms, doctor's plan, prescriptions and notes, questionnaire scores, medication adherence and side effects, body data, or meal photos) to the service you chose: OpenAI (through your ChatGPT plan) or a backup API you configured. Those services handle data under their own policies.
  • iCloud sync: visit notes, medications, sleep diary entries, summaries, body data, and app settings are end-to-end encrypted on your device before they are stored in your private iCloud database. The encryption key is kept automatically in your iCloud Keychain (itself end-to-end encrypted) and is available only to your devices signed in to the same Apple ID. iCloud holds only ciphertext that Apple, the developer, and anyone else cannot read.
  • Google Drive sync (not offered on the mainland China App Store): end-to-end encrypted on your device, then stored in the hidden app data folder of your Google Drive. The app cannot see your other Drive files.
  • PikPak WebDAV sync (not offered on the mainland China App Store): end-to-end encrypted on your device, then uploaded to the WebDAV address you enter.
  • Family view: you can invite family members to see a read-only sleep overview: the last 30 nights' sleep duration and scores, current medications and dose check-ins, the next visit date, questionnaire scores, and the symptoms, doctor's plan and prescription highlights from your latest visit. Sharing uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) sharing; the data stays in your own iCloud with its content fields encrypted, and the developer cannot read it. Family members can view but not edit. You can remove a family member or stop sharing at any time, and the copy on their devices is then deleted automatically.
  • Sharing and export: share images, the sleep diary PDF, visit summaries, and CSV exports are created only when you ask and are sent through the system share sheet to wherever you choose.

All sync methods use one encryption key: by default generated randomly by the app and kept only in your iCloud Keychain (end-to-end encrypted), available to your devices signed in to the same Apple ID; you can instead set your own sync password, kept only in each device's Keychain. Neither is ever uploaded to any cloud drive. Google and PikPak credentials stay in this device's Keychain and are never uploaded.

Where data is stored

The developer has no servers and stores none of your data. It is kept only in these places:

  • Your device: app data in the app's sandbox on your iPhone; API keys and sign-in credentials in the device Keychain.
  • Apple Health: managed by Apple on your device, and in iCloud if you sync Health with iCloud.
  • iCloud (if on): your own private iCloud database, end-to-end encrypted. For Apple Accounts in mainland China, iCloud is operated by GCBD and data is stored in China; elsewhere it is operated by Apple.
  • PikPak (if on; not offered on the mainland China App Store): encrypted, on the PikPak WebDAV server you enter; PikPak decides where it is located.
  • Google Drive (if on; not offered on the mainland China App Store): encrypted, on Google's servers, which may be outside China.
  • AI analysis: the on-device model never leaves the device. A custom API receives what you confirm sending, stored wherever that provider keeps it. ChatGPT / OpenAI (not offered on the mainland China App Store) are outside China.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, the app requests only openid and email (to show which account is signed in) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (to store the app's encrypted sync file in your Drive's app data folder). This data is used only to provide sync. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not transferred to others, not used to train AI models, and not read by humans unless you ask.

XUHERA's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion, and revoking access

You can delete records in the app; deletions sync to your other devices. Deleting the app removes its local data; Health data is managed in the Health app; cloud copies can be deleted in iCloud, Google Drive (Settings → Manage apps), or PikPak. You can sign out of Google in the app or revoke access at your Google Account permissions.

On first use you give separate consent to processing your health information (sensitive personal information). You can withdraw it at any time by turning off the app's access in iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices; this does not affect processing already done with your consent. You have the right to access, copy, correct and delete your personal information and to withdraw consent. As your data is kept on your own devices and cloud services, you can do this directly in the app, the Health app or the cloud service; for help, contact us at the address below.
