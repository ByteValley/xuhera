功能

  • 读取 Apple“健康”中的睡眠阶段，查看每晚和长期的睡眠趋势。
  • 记录复诊的症状、医生方案和处方，生成可导出 PDF 的“睡眠与症状摘要”。
  • 连接蓝牙体脂秤，记录体重、体脂率等身体数据，管理家庭成员、目标和饮食。
  • 可选的 AI 分析：优先使用本机模型；联网分析需要你每次确认。
  • 可选的多设备同步：iCloud、Google Drive 或 PikPak。数据上传前都会先在本机端到端加密，密钥自动保存在你的 iCloud 钥匙串中，无需设置密码。

隐私

应用没有自己的服务器，不含广告和数据分析工具。你的数据保存在你的 iPhone、Apple“健康”以及你自己选择的云服务中。详见隐私政策。

Sleep Health

Sleep Health is an iPhone app for sleep analysis, doctor-visit notes, and body measurements.

  • Reads sleep stages from Apple Health and shows nightly and long-term trends.
  • Keeps visit notes (symptoms, doctor's plan, prescriptions) and creates exportable PDF summaries.
  • Connects to Bluetooth body-fat scales to record weight and body composition; manages family members, goals, and meals.
  • Optional AI analysis, on-device first; online analysis only after you confirm each time.
  • Optional sync through iCloud, Google Drive, or PikPak. Data is always end-to-end encrypted on your device before upload, with the key kept in your iCloud Keychain, so no password is needed.

The app has no servers of its own and contains no ads or analytics. See the Privacy Policy. Contact: bytevalley@outlook.com
